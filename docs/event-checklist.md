# Event checklist for Suffolk Law student organizations

What it took to run a hybrid evening panel (networking, panel, reception, Zoom, student CLE credit) in fall 2026.
Dates count back from the event. Offices and forms change, so confirm each step with the office named.

## Six or more weeks out

- [ ] **Book the room** through the student organization room request in the My Suffolk portal. Popular rooms go early.
- [ ] **Pick a date and time** that works for evening students, and decide whether you're hybrid (room plus Zoom).
- [ ] **Line up speakers and a moderator.** Send each one the date, place, format and what you'd like them to cover.
- [ ] **Ask co-hosting organizations** early. Each one brings an audience and shares the promotion work.
- [ ] **Sort out the budget.** Food, drinks and speaker gifts go through your SBA budget and the Dean of Students office.
  Outside sponsors can donate, usually against an invoice, an ACH form and a W-9.

## Four weeks out

- [ ] **Make the RSVP form and short link** ([how](rsvp.md)) and a flyer with a QR code ([QR tool](../qr)).
- [ ] **Send a calendar hold** to speakers with the plan for the evening.
- [ ] **List the event** on your school's event calendar, and ask Professional & Career Development to include it in their listings.

## Two to three weeks out

- [ ] **Request student CLE credit** if the program qualifies. At Suffolk the request goes to the Associate Dean for
  Professional & Career Development **at least two weeks before** the event, with the event name, sponsors,
  your CLE coordinator, date and length (at least 60 minutes, live), format, panel, and how it meets the requirement.
- [ ] **Collect audience questions** from members and co-hosts and turn them into a short topic list for the speakers.
- [ ] **Set up Zoom and AV** with IT or media services: room mics, camera, the Zoom link.
- [ ] **Hold a 15-minute prep call** with the speakers: introductions, how the evening runs, topics, logistics. Record it for anyone who can't make it.

## One week out

- [ ] **Meet with the Dean of Students office** about the room setup and floor plan.
- [ ] **Register alcohol** if the reception serves it, using the school's alcohol event registration form.
- [ ] **Book media services** for photos or recording.
- [ ] **Write the run of show:** who speaks when, who introduces whom, who thanks whom.
- [ ] **Send the final topic list** and arrival details to speakers.

## Day of

- [ ] Sign-in table with printed sign-in sheets and name tags.
- [ ] Printed CLE attendance forms, filled in at the top and signed, handed out at the end.
- [ ] Someone running the Zoom and watching the chat.
- [ ] Someone taking photos.

## After

- [ ] Thank-you notes to speakers and co-hosts.
- [ ] Send CLE paperwork where it needs to go.
- [ ] Post photos and a recap.
- [ ] Add new sign-ins to your member list.
